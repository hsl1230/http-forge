# Legal notice / Impressum — template

> **Template only — not legal advice.** Replace every `[PLACEHOLDER]` before
> publishing. Do not publish this page with placeholders, a PO box, or a
> residential address you are not allowed to disclose. Have the final version
> reviewed by a qualified lawyer in your jurisdiction.

An Impressum is primarily a German-law requirement. If you are not established
in Germany, you may still need a legal notice or provider identification under
the law of your own country. The English version below is a generic starting
point; the German version is the § 5 DDG-style format.

## What to prepare

- Full legal name (or registered company name)
- A serviceable street address (not a PO box)
- Email address
- Phone number or another fast, direct contact channel
- Company register court and number, if applicable
- VAT ID (`USt-IdNr.`) or Wirtschafts-Identifikationsnummer, if assigned
- Name and address of the person responsible for editorial content, if
  applicable
- Privacy policy URL, especially if you use a newsletter or analytics

## Notes before publishing

1. **Use a real serviceable address.** A PO box or an unattended `c/o` address
   may not satisfy German requirements. If you do not want to publish your home
   address, use a business address or a rented Impressum address.
2. **Email alone may not be enough in Germany.** Provide a second fast contact
   channel, usually a phone number. Confirm the current requirement for your
   situation.
3. **Register and VAT details are conditional.** Include them only if they exist
   and apply to you. For sole traders, the relevant tax identifier may be the
   Wirtschafts-Identifikationsnummer rather than a VAT ID.
4. **The editorial-content line is conditional.** The `§ 18 Abs. 2 MStV`
   reference applies to journalistic-editorial offerings; not every blog needs
   it. Ask before adding it.
5. **An Impressum is not a privacy policy.** Hashnode, analytics, and newsletter
   tools process personal data. Publish a separate privacy policy and link it
   from the legal page.
6. **Do not copy another site's disclaimer verbatim.** Liability, dispute
   resolution, and consumer-law wording depend on your legal form and
   jurisdiction.
7. **Keep the page reachable.** Link the legal notice in the publication footer
   or navigation, not only in a single post.
